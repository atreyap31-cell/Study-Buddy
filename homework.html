💡 How this works

Type an assignment, choose how often it comes around, and set what it is worth. The clock updates every second so you always know exactly how long is left — green means plenty of time, yellow means it is getting close, red means within a day (or already late).

Repeating work (reading every night, spelling every Friday) ticks off one day at a time: finish today's and the countdown rolls on to the next one. Check the 🔁 Repeating filter to see everything on a schedule, and the 📅 Calendar to see it day by day.

Everything saves automatically to this browser.
